[H2] What is RERA and Why Does It Matter?

The **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016** is a landmark legislation that has transformed India's real estate sector. In Rajasthan, RERA is implemented by **RERA Rajasthan**, providing comprehensive protection to homebuyers.

[H2] Key Benefits of RERA for Homebuyers

[H3] 1. Project Registration Mandatory

Every real estate project above 500 sq.m. or more than 8 apartments must be registered with RERA. This ensures transparency and accountability from developers.

[H3] 2. 70% Funds in Escrow

Developers must deposit 70% of project funds in a separate escrow account, ensuring money is used only for construction purposes.

[H3] 3. No Pre-Launch Sales

Properties can only be marketed and sold after RERA registration, preventing fraudulent schemes.

[H3] 4. Carpet Area Transparency

Pricing must be based on carpet area, not super built-up area, giving buyers accurate information about usable space.

[H3] 5. Timely Delivery Guarantee

Developers face penalties for delayed possession, ensuring projects are completed on schedule.

[H2] How to Verify RERA Registration

1. Visit the official website: **rera.rajasthan.gov.in**
2. Click on "Registered Projects"
3. Enter the RERA registration number
4. Verify project details, approvals, and developer information

[H2] Desire Realty: 100% RERA Compliant

• **Brajeshwar Avenue:** RAJ/P/2025/4285
